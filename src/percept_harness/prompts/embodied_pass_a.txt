- name is one concise English canonical_label for one visible or action-relevant entity; name must not normalize to unknown after whitespace and case normalization.
- aliases is a JSON list of concise English alternative labels. Aliases must be non-blank and unique after whitespace and case normalization.
- role must be exactly one of: actor, manipulated_object, container, occluder, surface, other.
- Include visible actors and visible manipulated objects needed by the action descriptions. Include a container, occluder, or surface only when it is visible or action-relevant.
- Candidates are evidence requests, not claims of presence. They request later visual tracking; they do not prove that an entity exists, is visible throughout, or is occluded.

[coarse action rules]
- Each action is one meaningful physical phase.
- Use visible evidence only. Do not infer hidden intent.
- Prefer boundaries where the manipulated object, held state, contact state, destination interaction, or idle/active phase changes.
- Do not create one-second grids.
- Mark only intervals where a visible action is actually happening. Idle stretches, an empty scene, or a fully static frame get no action: leave those intervals uncovered rather than inventing a filler action.
- Actions must be chronological and non-overlapping, and must stay within 0.0 through video_duration_seconds. They need not touch each other and need not start at 0.0 or end at the video end.
- Every action must have positive duration. action_index values must be unique and strictly increasing.
- Use seconds relative to the complete video.
