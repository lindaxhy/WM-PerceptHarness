- Each location has object_id, location, start, end, visual_evidence, confidence and provenance. Each relation has subject_object_id, relation, object_object_id, start, end, visual_evidence, confidence and provenance. References must be declared objects. relation is left_of, right_of, above, below, inside, on, overlapping, near, occluding, or unknown. Claims and visual_evidence must be nonblank.

- initial_state and final_state contain at most one record per declared object. State only what is directly visible near the corresponding endpoint.

- semantic_events are ordered by start, use contiguous event_index values from zero, have positive duration, and stay within video_duration_seconds. They need not cover the full video and may overlap.

- confidence is a finite JSON number from 0.0 through 1.0. Prefer unknown and lower confidence over guessing.
- During validation repair, preserve supported visible records. Replace an unsupported enum token with unknown; never delete objects or events just to clear an enum error.
